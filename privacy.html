Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

Stone Trace (“Stone Trace,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting information collected through the Stone Trace application and related services.

Information We Collect

Stone Trace may collect information that users voluntarily provide or configure within the application, including:

  • Name or account information
  • Mobile telephone number
  • Email account configuration information necessary to process authorized messages
  • Geographic coordinates and location information contained in authorized data sources
  • User-created geofences, labels, target names, and notification settings
  • Application configuration and operational information
  • SMS notification preferences and consent records

Stone Trace processes this information to provide the application's location monitoring, geofence, notification, and related functionality.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain Stone Trace;
  • Process authorized location information;
  • Determine whether configured location information enters or exits user-defined geographic areas;
  • Generate requested application and SMS notifications;
  • Maintain user preferences and configurations;
  • Provide technical support;
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse; and
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

SMS Communications

Users may voluntarily opt in to receive automated SMS notifications from Stone Trace regarding configured location and geofence events.

Message frequency varies depending on configured alerts and activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Users may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP. Users may reply HELP for assistance.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent information will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may be provided to telecommunications and service providers, such as SMS delivery providers, solely as necessary to provide requested messaging and application functionality.

Location Information

Stone Trace may process geographic coordinates or other location-related information supplied through data sources authorized and configured by the user. Location information is used to perform requested application functions, including determining whether configured coordinates are inside or outside user-defined geographic areas and generating corresponding notifications.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their collection, access, processing, and use of location information complies with applicable laws, policies, agreements, and authorization requirements.

Information Sharing

Stone Trace does not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed to service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the service, including hosting, communications, security, and SMS delivery providers. These providers receive information only as necessary to perform services on behalf of Stone Trace.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

Data Security

Stone Trace uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Stone Trace service, satisfy legitimate operational requirements, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

User Choices

Recipients may stop SMS notifications by replying STOP. Administrators may also disable alert delivery to a recipient in Stone Trace.

Users may also contact Stone Trace regarding questions about their information or requests concerning applicable privacy rights.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date displayed above.
