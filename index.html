Stone Trace

Stone Trace processes authorized location reports and provides optional automated SMS notifications to employees assigned to receive configured alerts.

SMS notification program

Alerts report entry into or exit from configured geofences and location reports whose accuracy radius exceeds a configured threshold. Message frequency varies based on configured alerts and activity. Message and data rates may apply.

To enroll, open the consent form, enter your mobile number, select the SMS consent checkbox, and submit. Consent is recorded before Stone Trace permits SMS delivery. Administrator assignment is also required to receive alerts. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Open SMS consent form

Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

SMS privacy

Mobile information and messaging consent records are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers may process information as necessary to operate the service and deliver requested messages. See our Privacy Policy for details.
